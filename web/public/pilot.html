Every exception explained.

Pseudonymized events from our own stores (Equilibrium and partner stores on the Brazil-Paraguay border) stream into PixSettle. Orders are reconciled against each Pix charge; every exception gets a verdict with evidence: deterministic rules first, the copilot model only for the residue, a human for anything that moves money.

No names, documents, contacts, addresses or products leave the store. Ids are pseudonymous and letters only. Nothing here changes the store's checkout or money flow.
